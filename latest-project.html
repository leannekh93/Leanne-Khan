Case Study: Improving lead capture response for field service providers with PestBot

For local service providers, managing an incoming lead while out on the field can be frustrating. Sometimes that includes online form coordination, upfront deposits, calendar booking and follow-up review requests across entirely separate applications.

Key Friction Points
  • Operators successfully answer the first two calls of the morning. Once they are on-site and executing jobs, the remaining 80% of daily inbound calls go unanswered.
  • Service providers rely on a fractured tech stack. Juggling separate, disconnected applications for invoicing, scheduling, and lead generation creates administrative chaos.
  • There is a severe channel misalignment. While operators use complex backend systems, the local consumer base exclusively demands friction-free communication via WhatsApp.

The solution: A proactive 3-level system

PestBot solves this fragmentation by turning those isolated, confusing tasks into a single automated pipeline. Each level solves a different bottleneck, allowing solo service providers to focus on pest control while the bot handles the admin.

Level 1

Intelligent Intake & Qualification

Instantly captures incoming website leads, sanitizes phone data, logs them into the CRM, and uses a logic switch to automatically separate standard fixed-rate requests from unique custom assessments.

Level 2

Secure Payment & Calendar Sync

Bridges the gap between payment and scheduling by embedding a unique tracking ID into a secure PayFast checkout link — automatically updating CRM status and confirming the Google Calendar slot the exact second a deposit is verified.

Level 3

Automated Engagement

Runs a daily evening check that, if a job is specifically completed that day, automatically sends a WhatsApp review request — targeting only eligible clients without slowing down database performance.

Results …

  • 60% Lead Rate Automated and secured 6 out of 10 incoming customer leads in a single 8-hour shift.
  • +3 Bookings Generated 3 extra revenue-producing jobs automatically without lifting a finger.
  • 0% Friction Flawless PayFast tracking integration with zero deposit or booking loop errors.
  • Zero-Stress Setup An incredibly fast learning curve mastered by the business owner in just a few tries.
  • Smart Fallback Successfully caught and flagged complex client messages for easy manual follow-up.

I enjoy it because I already use WhatsApp and I don't have to force clients to reach me on a website — they have access to me this way. It also makes it easy for me to market because my leads are automatically booked. I don't worry about getting them onto a list, it's already done for me. Love it!

— Pieter Van der Heever, KOSH Pest Control
